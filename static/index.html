A QUICK WALKTHROUGH

Guess. Learn.
Keep the receipt.

  1. Start a round. The service picks one of 22 five-letter crypto terms and commits a salted hash to Aptos devnet.
  2. Guess from the word list. Green is an exact match, amber is a misplaced letter, and grey means no unused copy of that letter remains. Repeated letters count only as often as they appear in the answer.
  3. Find the word within six guesses. Each submitted guess sends an Aptos devnet transaction.
  4. When you win, use all six guesses, or end the round, the service sends the answer and salt for verification. Move checks the commitment and every feedback row before updating your round record.

No wallet extension needed to play. Keys are held by the Python server. The app needs an internet connection to play.
